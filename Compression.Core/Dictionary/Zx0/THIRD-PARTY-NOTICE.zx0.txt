ZX0 (Einar Saukas) — stream format followed from the reference compressor

Source:   https://github.com/einar-saukas/ZX0 (src/compress.c, src/dzx0.c)
Revision: the revision consulted was not recorded; the licence below was
          verified at ecde3a2ae05061fe06469ed46df81a33b7de7d86 on 2026-10-19.
Author:   Einar Saukas
Licence:  BSD-3-Clause (reproduced below)

Zx0BuildingBlock.cs reproduces the ZX0 v2 forward bit stream (interlaced
Elias-gamma codes, the offset-LSB "backtrack" bit, the 256 end marker) as the
reference compressor emits it. The encoder is a greedy parser of this
project's own; the reference's optimal parser is not reproduced.
