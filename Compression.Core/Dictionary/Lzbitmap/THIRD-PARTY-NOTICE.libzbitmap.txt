libzbitmap (LZBITMAP) — converted to C#

Source:   https://github.com/eafer/libzbitmap
Revision: 574abeae25b25c3319b9b6a9225cc7464e08ae88
Author:   Ernesto A. Fernández, Corellium LLC
Licence:  MIT (reproduced below)

Lzbitmap.cs is a conversion of libzbitmap.c (zbm_decompress, zbm_compress)
into this codebase, not a verbatim copy. The format constants (the "ZBM\x09"
magic, the 32 KiB chunk limit, the 6- and 15-byte chunk headers, twelve
10-bit top bitmaps in 17 bytes, bitmap numbers 0-2 / 3-14 / 0xF, repetition
counts from 4) are copied exactly, as the format requires. The compressor
keeps libzbitmap's pattern search and top-bitmap selection.
