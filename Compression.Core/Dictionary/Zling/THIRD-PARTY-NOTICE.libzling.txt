ZlingCodec.cs is a C# conversion of libzling (src/libzling.cpp, libzling_lz.cpp,
libzling_huffman.cpp and the generated tables in src/tables/):

  libzling, Zhang Li
  https://github.com/richox/libzling
  revision 6828e31517bf94fda8e1e8e15a583ab58acde637

Local changes: the Huffman tree is built with an explicit copy of libstdc++'s binary-heap
algorithms so ties break as in the reference built with GCC; errors are InvalidDataException;
the decoder checks match positions and block bounds instead of relying on sentinel space.
