BalzCodec.cs is a C# conversion of:

  balz.cpp, BALZ 1.20, Ilya Muravyov (2015)
  http://sourceforge.net/projects/balz/  (balz120src.zip)

Licence, as stated in the file: "balz.cpp is written and placed in the public domain by Ilya
Muravyov".

Local changes: none to the file format. The per-context position tables are allocated on first
use rather than as one 32 MiB array; the decoder refuses truncated input and matches that point
outside the block decoded so far instead of reading past its buffer.
