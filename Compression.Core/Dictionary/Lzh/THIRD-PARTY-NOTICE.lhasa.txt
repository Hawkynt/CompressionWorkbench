Files converted (decoders only; lhasa has no encoders):

  lib/lh1_decoder.c                 -> Lh1Tree.cs, Lh1Decoder.cs
  lib/lzs_decoder.c                 -> LzsDecoder.cs
  lib/lz5_decoder.c                 -> Lz5Decoder.cs (initial ring contents copied exactly)
  lib/pm1_decoder.c                 -> Pm1Decoder.cs (tables copied exactly)
  lib/pm2_decoder.c, tree_decode.c  -> Pm2Decoder.cs (tables copied exactly)
  lib/pma_common.c                  -> PmaHistory.cs
  lib/bit_stream_reader.c           -> LhaBitInput.cs

The -lh1-, -lzs- and -lz5- encoders (Lh1Encoder.cs, LzsEncoder.cs, Lz5Encoder.cs)
are our own, built to the bit streams these decoders read and verified by lhasa
extracting what they write.
