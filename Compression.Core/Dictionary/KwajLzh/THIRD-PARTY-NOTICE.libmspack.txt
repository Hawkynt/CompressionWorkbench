KwajLzhBuildingBlock.Decode is a conversion of the method-3 decoder above,
including its end-of-input rule (zero bits may be read past the end; the token
that consumed them is dropped; decoding stops after the token during which the
end was first touched). The encoder is original to this repository; libmspack
has no KWAJ compressor.

The method-2 decoder in FileFormat.Kwaj (KwajStream.DecompressLzss) follows
libmspack/mspack/lzssd.c in LZSS_MODE_QBASIC.
