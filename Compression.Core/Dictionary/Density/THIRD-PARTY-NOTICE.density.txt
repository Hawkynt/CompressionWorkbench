DensityCodec.cs is a C# conversion of density-rs 0.16.6 (src/codec/codec.rs,
src/codec/protection_state.rs and src/algorithms/{chameleon,cheetah,lion}):

  density, Guillaume Voirin
  https://github.com/g1mv/density
  revision 58938943bb0dfd85b186551a3d1d36cc286f9c1d

Licensed under MIT OR Apache-2.0; used here under the MIT licence.

Local changes: errors are InvalidDataException; the decoder grows its output instead of writing
into a caller-sized buffer.
