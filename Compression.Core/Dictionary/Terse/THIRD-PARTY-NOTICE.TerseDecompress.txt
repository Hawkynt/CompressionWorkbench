TerseCodec's header parser and its PACK and SPACK decoders are conversions of the
files above. The PACK encoder is original to this repository.

Modifications: converted to C#; the decoders report malformed input as
InvalidDataException instead of writing what they can; only binary mode is
implemented (no EBCDIC-to-ASCII text conversion).
