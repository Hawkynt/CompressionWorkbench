Rar3Decoder.cs was converted from that file. It covers the bit stream, the table and
precode reading, the LZ symbol set, filter parsing, the RarVM program fingerprints, and
the delta, E8/E8E9, RGB and audio filters. Rar3Encoder.cs writes the same format.
libarchive does not cover three behaviours: the IA-64 filter, the end-of-file
marker in solid streams, and the low-offset reset when tables are read. Each was
written down from reading unrar 7.0.9 (rarvm.cpp, unpack30.cpp). The unRAR licence
rules out its code, so that is sourcing rung 2 and no code was carried across. They
were then implemented independently and checked against RAR 6.24 archives.
