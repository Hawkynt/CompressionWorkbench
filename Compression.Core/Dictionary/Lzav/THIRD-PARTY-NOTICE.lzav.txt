LZAV — converted

LzavCodec.cs converts lzav_compress (with lzav_compress_default's choice of minimum
reference length), lzav_write_blk_3, lzav_write_fin_3, lzav_match_len, lzav_match_len_r1
and lzav_decompress_3. Word-sized copies are replaced by byte copies; the stream is the
same.
