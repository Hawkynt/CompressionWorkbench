  XADARJFastestHandle.m  -> ArjFastestDecoder.cs

The method-4 decoder is a conversion, not a copy: the token grammar (unary-
prefixed length and distance codes, minimum match 3) is carried across; the
CSHandle/LZSS framework around it is not. Checked against archives written by
the open-source ARJ port (arj a -m4).
