lzfse (Apple's reference LZFSE/LZVN implementation) — LZVN decoder converted to C#

Source:   https://github.com/lzfse/lzfse
Revision: e634ca58b4821d9f3d560cdc6df5dec02ffc93fd
Licence:  BSD-3-Clause (reproduced below)

Lzvn.Decompress is a conversion of src/lzvn_decode_base.c into this codebase,
not a verbatim copy: the opcode table (sml_d, med_d, lrg_d, pre_d, sml_l,
lrg_l, sml_m, lrg_m, eos, nop, undefined) and the bit layout of each opcode are
copied exactly, as the format requires. Lzvn.Compress keeps its own match
finder and emits only opcodes that table defines.
