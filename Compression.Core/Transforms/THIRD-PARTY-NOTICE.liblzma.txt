BcjFilter.cs carries these conversions of liblzma filters into this codebase:

  src/liblzma/simple/armthumb.c   -> TransformArmThumb
  src/liblzma/simple/arm64.c      -> TransformArm64
  src/liblzma/simple/riscv.c      -> RiscVEncode / RiscVDecode
  src/liblzma/simple/x86.c        -> TransformX86 (converted 2026-10-30 from tag
                                     v5.8.4, d3e650e63c110e830fd5391e7f8b45df0b91d3da)
  src/liblzma/simple/sparc.c      -> TransformSparc (same revision)
  src/liblzma/simple/ia64.c       -> TransformIA64 (same revision)

The ARM and PowerPC filters in the same file make no conversion claim; this
notice records only the conversions above. The x86, SPARC and IA-64 filters
were converted after they were found to disagree with liblzma and 7-Zip on
real data (BcjLiblzmaVectorTests now pins them to liblzma's output).
