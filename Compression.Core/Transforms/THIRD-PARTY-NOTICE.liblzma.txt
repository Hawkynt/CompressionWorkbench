BcjFilter.cs carries three conversions of liblzma filters into this codebase:

  src/liblzma/simple/armthumb.c   -> TransformArmThumb
  src/liblzma/simple/arm64.c      -> TransformArm64
  src/liblzma/simple/riscv.c      -> RiscVEncode / RiscVDecode

The x86, ARM, PowerPC, SPARC and IA-64 filters in the same file make no
conversion claim; this notice records only the three conversions above.
