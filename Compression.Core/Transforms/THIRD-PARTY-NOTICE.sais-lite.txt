SuffixArraySais.cs is a conversion of sais.c's getCounts, getBuckets, LMSsort1,
LMSpostproc1, induceSA and sais_main into this codebase. It always takes the
LMSsort1 path and allocates its bucket arrays and the reduced problem
separately rather than inside spare suffix-array space; the suffix array it
produces is the same.
