Converted parts, into Compression.Core/Crypto/AesBlockCipher.cs:
- AesEngine: the S, Si, T0 and Tinv0 tables (byte for byte), the round function
  over one rotated table per direction, the final rounds from S and Si, and the
  InvMixColumns of the decryption round keys (FFmulX, FFmulX2, Inv_Mcol).
- AesEngine_X86: the AESKEYGENASSIST key schedules for 128-, 192- and 256-bit
  keys and the inverse round keys through AESIMC.
The FIPS-197 key expansion is written as one loop over the key length instead
of the engine's three unrolled ones, and the eight-block pipeline over the AES
instructions is written as one loop over the rounds.

Converted parts, into Compression.Core/Crypto/TripleDes.cs:
- DesEngine: the bytebit, bigbyte, pc1, totrot, pc2 and SP1-SP8 tables (value
  for value), GenerateWorkingKey and DesFunc, its two half-rounds per loop
  written as one round function.
- DesEdeEngine: the encrypt-decrypt-encrypt order, the third key of a 16-byte
  bundle being the first.
