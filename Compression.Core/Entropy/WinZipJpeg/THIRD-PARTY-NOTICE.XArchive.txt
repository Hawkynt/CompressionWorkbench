WinZipJpegDecoder.cs converts that decoder: the properties and bundle headers, the
LZMA metadata, the marker parser, the log-domain arithmetic decoder, the coefficient
model, and the Huffman re-encoder. WinZipJpegTables.cs copies its log/antilog
tables exactly. The decoder follows WinZip's "JPEG Compression - Method 96"
specification (2008) and U.S. patent 4,791,403, with corrections to the printed
formulas that real WinZip streams require. WinZipJpegEncoder.cs is our own inverse of
that decoder. It uses the same model and tables, and an arithmetic encoder that mirrors
the decoder.
