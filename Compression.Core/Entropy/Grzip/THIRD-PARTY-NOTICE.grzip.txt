GrzipCodec is a conversion of the functions above; the constants (signature,
model rates, WFC weights and positions, group tables) are copied exactly. The
encoder follows libgrzip's LZP + BWT + MTF path, using this repository's own
Burrows-Wheeler transform for the sort.

Modifications: converted to C#; malformed input raises InvalidDataException
instead of reading past buffers.
