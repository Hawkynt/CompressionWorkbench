FreeArcGrzip.cs is a conversion of the decoding half of that code: the block
decoder (stored, record-filtered and sorted blocks), the inverse BWT (fast and
strong sorting), the inverse ST4, the MTF and WFC rank decoders with their
arithmetic coder and model constants, the LZP decoder and the record filter
decoder. FreeArc's multi-threaded stream wrapper around it was not taken; the
block sequence it reads is documented in docs/FREEARC-ON-DISK.md.
