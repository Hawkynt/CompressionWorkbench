kwaj-lzh — KWAJ method 2 and method 3 decoders converted to C#

Source: https://github.com/yaodub/kwaj-lzh
Revision: f71544bc4f848420d8aedb46c2ae602b88a3e998 (0.1.0)
Files: src/kwaj.ts (LZH decoder), src/bitreader.ts, src/lzss.ts (QBASIC mode)

KwajLzhDecoder and KwajStream.DecompressLzss convert these MIT-licensed decoders.
The conversion uses C# spans and streams, validates complete Huffman tables,
and reports malformed tables with InvalidDataException. The KWAJ encoder is
original to this repository. libmspack is retained only as a black-box oracle.
