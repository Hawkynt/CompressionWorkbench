Files converted:

  src/huffman/huff.cpp   -> MpqHuffman.cs (weight tables copied exactly)
  src/adpcm/adpcm.cpp    -> MpqAdpcm.cs (step tables copied exactly)
  src/sparse/sparse.cpp  -> MpqSparse.cs
  src/SCompression.cpp   -> MpqCompression.cs (mask order and the version-2 mask set)
  src/SBaseCommon.cpp    -> MpqCrypto.cs (name hashing rules, file-key derivation
                            and key recovery)

The PKWARE DCL codec these use lives in Compression.Core/Dictionary/PkwareDcl
with its own copy of this notice.
