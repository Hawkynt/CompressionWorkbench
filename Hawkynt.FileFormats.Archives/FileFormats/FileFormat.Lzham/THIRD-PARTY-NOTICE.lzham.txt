Converted into this directory:

  lzhamdecomp/lzham_lzdecomp.cpp, lzham_symbol_codec.h (decoding macros)
                                         -> LzhamDecoder.cs
  lzhamdecomp/lzham_symbol_codec.cpp, lzham_huffman_codes.cpp,
  lzham_prefix_coding.cpp, lzham_lzdecompbase.cpp (tables)
                                         -> LzhamModels.cs
  lzhamdecomp/lzham_symbol_codec.cpp (encoding side),
  lzhamcomp/lzham_lzcomp_state.cpp (state::encode, encode_eob),
  lzhamcomp/lzham_lzcomp_internal.cpp (block framing, raw-block fallback,
  final block)                           -> LzhamEncoder.cs

The match search and parse in LzhamEncoder.cs (hash chains, one step of
lazy evaluation) are this repository's own; LZHAM's optimal and extreme
parsers and its match accelerator were not converted.

LzhamStream.cs follows the file layout written by lzhamtest/lzhamtest.cpp
(id "LZH0", dictionary size log2, 64-bit little-endian length).
