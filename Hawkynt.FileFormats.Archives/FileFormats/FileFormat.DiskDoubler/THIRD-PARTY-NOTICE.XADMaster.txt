Files converted:

  XADDiskDoublerParser.m    -> DiskDoublerReader.cs (header layout, method
                               numbering, XOR masks, checksums, the method 7
                               table, the method 8 prefix, and the DDAR and
                               DDA2 archive layouts)
  XADDiskDoublerADnHandle.m -> DiskDoublerAdDecoder.cs
  XADDiskDoublerMethod2Handle.m, XADStacLZSHandle.m, XADDeltaHandle.m
                            -> DiskDoublerCodecs.cs (decoders; the encoders
                               there are our own, written against them)

Methods 3, 4 and 10 and delta types other than 1 are refused rather than
decoded. Method 8 uses the Compact Pro decoder in FileFormat.CompactPro with
DiskDoubler's 0xFFF0-unit LZH blocks. The header CRC is CRC-16/XMODEM over the 82 bytes before it (XADMaster
computes the same value through a bit-reversed table).
