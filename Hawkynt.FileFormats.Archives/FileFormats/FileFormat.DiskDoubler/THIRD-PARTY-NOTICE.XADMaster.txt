Files converted:

  XADDiskDoublerParser.m    -> DiskDoublerReader.cs (header layout, method
                               numbering, compress-method XOR and checksum)
  XADDiskDoublerADnHandle.m -> DiskDoublerAdDecoder.cs

Methods 2-5, 7, 8 and 10 and delta pre-processing are refused rather than
decoded. The header CRC is CRC-16/XMODEM over the 82 bytes before it (XADMaster
computes the same value through a bit-reversed table).
