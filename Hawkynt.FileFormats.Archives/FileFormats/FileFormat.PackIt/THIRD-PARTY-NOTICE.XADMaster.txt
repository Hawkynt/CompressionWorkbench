Files converted:

  XADPackItParser.m         -> PackItReader.cs (entry layout)
  XADStuffItHuffmanHandle.m -> PackItHuffmanDecoder.cs

The header CRC (which XADMaster reads but does not check) was identified by
measurement: CRC-16/XMODEM over the 92 header bytes before it matched every
header of nine real PackIt archives. Encrypted entries (PMa5/PMa6) are listed
and refused rather than decrypted.
