Xp3Reader.cs is a conversion of the archive-opening half of ArcXP3.cs
(header, 2.32 continuation index, raw/zlib index, File/info/segm/adlr
chunks, multi-segment members) into this codebase; Xp3Writer.cs follows the
layout of ArcXP3.cs's Create. GARbro's game-specific encryption schemes were
not taken. Both were then checked against archives written by the KiriKiri 2
Releaser and against the KiriKiri Z engine itself.
