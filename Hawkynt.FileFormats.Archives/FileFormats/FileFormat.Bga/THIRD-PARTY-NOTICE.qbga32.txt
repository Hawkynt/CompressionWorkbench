QBga32 (k.inaba) — GZA/BZA record layout and checksum converted to C#

Source:   http://www.kmonos.net/repos/qbga32 (Fossil), trunk tarball of 2015-07-03,
          version 0.06, file src/bga_melter.d
Author:   k.inaba (kmonos.net)
Licence:  NYSL Version 0.9982 (the project readme: "他は NYSL 0.9982 に基づいてご自由に";
          QBga32's bundled zlib/libbzip2 binaries were not used)

BgaArchive.cs is a conversion of bga_melter.d's header structure, header search,
signed-byte checksum, stored-or-compressed rule and gzip/bzip2 member handling.
NYSL A-4 asks that a modified version name its publisher: this conversion is part
of CompressionWorkbench (https://github.com/Hawkynt/CompressionWorkbench).
