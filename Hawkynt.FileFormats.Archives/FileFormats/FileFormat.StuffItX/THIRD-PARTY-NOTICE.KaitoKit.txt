StuffIt X encryption (StuffItXCrypto.cs) and method 7, JPEG recompression (StuffItXJpeg*.cs), in
this folder are converted from KaitoKit; the tables in StuffItXJpegTables.cs are its tables, and
the test streams in Compression.Tests/StuffItX/JpegVectors come from its JPEG fixture.
