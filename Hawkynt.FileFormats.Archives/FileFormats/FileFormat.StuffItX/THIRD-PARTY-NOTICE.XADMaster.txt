The PPMd variant G algorithm is by Dmitry Shkarin.

Tables copied verbatim: the PPMd binary-escape initialisers, the escape table, the Cyanide Markov
context groups. Everything else was rewritten in C# for this codebase.

Local changes against XADMaster's behaviour, each confirmed against StuffIt-written archives:
- catalog tag 10: the name strings start on a byte boundary after the count (XADMaster reads them
  unaligned and fails on StuffIt 7 for Mac archives);
- a data stream is decoded to the size its data element records, which the stored CRC-32 covers,
  even where its forks claim less (the preview stream of StuffIt Deluxe 2009/2010 archives);
- Iron's weighted recency list stops sinking an entry at the last of its 256 slots, where
  XADMaster reads one element past its array.
