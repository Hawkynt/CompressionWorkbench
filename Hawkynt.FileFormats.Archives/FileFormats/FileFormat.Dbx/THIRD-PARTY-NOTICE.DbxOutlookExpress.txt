DbxOutlookExpress (James John McGuire) — DBX index-tree, indexed-item and message-chain reading

Source:   https://github.com/jamesjohnmcguire/DbxOutlookExpress
Revision: master as fetched 2026-10-26
Licence:  MIT (reproduced below)

DbxReader.cs follows that library's reading of the DBX layout (header dwords 0x31/0x39/0x3B, tree
nodes of 0x27C bytes, indexed items with inline/offset attributes, message block chains) together
with Arne Schloh's published description of the format; it is written for this codebase, not a copy.
