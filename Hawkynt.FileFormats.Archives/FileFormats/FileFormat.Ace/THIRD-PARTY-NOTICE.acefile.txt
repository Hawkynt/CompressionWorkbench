The ACE decoder (AceBitStream, AceHuffman, AceLz77, AceSound, AcePic, AceDecompressor, AceBlowfish)
and the ACE header parsing in Hawkynt.FileFormats.Archives/FileFormats/FileFormat.Ace/AceReader.cs
are converted to C# from acefile:

  Upstream: https://github.com/droe/acefile
  Revision: 11ed076ae19e8228148731cfbc05f019a395b3b9 (acefile.py)
  Licence:  BSD-2-Clause

AceCompressor and the tree writer in AceHuffman are our own encoder, written against that decoder.
