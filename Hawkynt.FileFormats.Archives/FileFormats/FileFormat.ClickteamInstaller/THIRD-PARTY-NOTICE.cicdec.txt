ClickteamReader.cs is converted from cicdec, the Clickteam Install Creator unpacker:

  https://github.com/Bioruebe/cicdec
  revision 42c3c9d030d75ed9096e3c84ef5ffd19bde51b37 (release 3.0.1)
  files: cicdec/Program.cs, cicdec/FileInfo.cs

The block walk, the file list layouts 2.0, 2.4, 3.0, 3.5 and 4.0, the layout probe and the member
encoding come from it. Decompression uses this repository's own DEFLATE and bzip2 decoders.
