The version 6 reading of DxArchiveReader.cs (header and index layout, the password key, the key
recovered from the header, and the LZ decoder in DxCodecs.cs) is converted from GARbro:

  https://github.com/morkt/GARbro
  revision b09ee4570ccb1daf6ac56710ee8934dc0b8baeb0
  files: ArcFormats/DxLib/ArcDX.cs, ArcFormats/DxLib/DxKey.cs

Version 8 is implemented from docs/DXA-ON-DISK.md, which was written from the archiver source that
DxLib ships with its tools; no code was taken from it.
