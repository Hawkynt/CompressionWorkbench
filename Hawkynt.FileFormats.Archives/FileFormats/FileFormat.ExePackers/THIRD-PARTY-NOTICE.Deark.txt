The DOS executable unpackers in this folder are C# conversions of Deark's decoders:

  LzExeUnpacker.cs   modules/lzexe.c   (LZEXE 0.90 / 0.91 / 0.91e)
  PkLiteUnpacker.cs  modules/pklite.c  (PKLITE EXE and COM)
  DosExe.cs          the 28-byte header writer of the same modules and the entry-point
                     fingerprint of src/fmtutil-exe.c
  ExePackRelocations src/fmtutil-cmpr.c, fmtutil_decompress_exepack_reloc_tbl

from:

  Deark, Jason Summers
  https://github.com/jsummers/deark
  revision add9602d6cf77a189fc2762ed13077431dd7016e

Local changes: errors are InvalidDataException; reads past the end of the file fail instead
of returning zeros; the LHARK-SFX, PCX2EXE and CEBE COM variants and the raw-output mode
are not carried over; PKLITE's version fingerprinting and min/max-allocation research
code are not carried over.
