DosTracer.cs recognises the end of a DOS decompression stub with the rule of

  IUP 0.6.7 (Intelligent Executable Unpacker), Frank Zago, 1996-08-07

which IUP's documentation places in the public domain ("IUP executable and source code is
public domain. Feel free to use it or modify it as you wish."): a jump backwards by at least
2000 bytes, or to offset 0x100, taken while DS and ES hold the PSP segment, ends the stub; a
long forward jump starts a new phase. The 8086/80186 emulator (DosCpu.cs), the image-length
rules and the relocation recovery by two runs are this repository's own.
