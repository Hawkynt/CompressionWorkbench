GARbro (morkt) — LiveMaker VF archive layout and chunk scramble converted to C#

LiveMakerReader.cs converts ArcVF.cs's directory reader (TpRandom name and
offset obfuscation) and its LiveMaker Pro chunk scramble (TpScramble,
ReshuffleStream) into this codebase. The three per-member arrays after the
method bytes, the CRC-32 rule and the writer's layout were measured on a save
archive LiveMaker itself wrote and checked against pylivemaker
(GPL-3.0-or-later), which served only as an oracle; none of its code was taken.
