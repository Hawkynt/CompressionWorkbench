What was taken: the keying material (password, archive and stream headers),
the subheader layout, the PBKDF2 hash and output split per cipher, and the
choice of EAX or CTR+HMAC. The C# is an altered version written for this
repository; EAX itself follows Bellare, Rogaway and Wagner (FSE 2004) in
Compression.Core/Crypto/Eax.cs.
