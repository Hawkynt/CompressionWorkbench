binwalk v3 (ReFirmLabs) — TRX header layout and checksum converted to C#

Source:   https://github.com/ReFirmLabs/binwalk
Revision: 26713972e3f9f52dc37d4a421c4554b0bd9e82ef (checked against when this notice was written)
Licence:  MIT (reproduced below)

TrxImage is a conversion into this codebase of the TRX header structure and checksum rule in
src/structures/trx.rs and src/extractors/trx.rs, not a verbatim copy. The magic, field offsets and
CRC parameters are copied exactly, as the format requires. binwalk v3 is also run as a black-box
oracle in the test suite.
