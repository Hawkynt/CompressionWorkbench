AOSP Android Verified Boot (libavb / avbtool) — vbmeta structures converted to C#

Source:   https://android.googlesource.com/platform/external/avb
Revision: c5066a96caa7bf4150c0a8cc8cc14ab81733fdc7
Licence:  MIT (the licence of libavb/avb_vbmeta_image.h, libavb/avb_descriptor.h and avbtool.py;
          reproduced below)

AvbVbmeta and AvbDescriptorText are a conversion into this codebase of the vbmeta header, the
descriptor structures and the public-key encoding defined in those files, not a verbatim copy.
Magic strings, field offsets, descriptor tags and algorithm numbers are copied exactly, as the
format requires. avbtool at the same revision is also run as a black-box oracle in the test suite.
