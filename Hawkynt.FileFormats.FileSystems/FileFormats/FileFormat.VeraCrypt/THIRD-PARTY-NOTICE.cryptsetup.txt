Converted parts: the key-derivation table (PRFs, iteration counts, PIM
constants), the cipher-chain table (key and IV offsets per cipher, XTS, LRW
and legacy CBC modes), header decryption for those modes, the keyfile pool
and the header/backup/hidden header offsets. The sector-level legacy modes
(tcw IV and whitening) are not in this file; they are specified in
docs/VERACRYPT-ON-DISK.md from the behaviour of the cryptsetup test images.
