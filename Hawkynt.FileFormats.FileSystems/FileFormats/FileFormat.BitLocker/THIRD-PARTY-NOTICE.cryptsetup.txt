Converted parts: the on-disk structures (boot-sector signatures, BITLK
superblock, FVE metadata block and metadata headers, validation block, VMK and
key entries, .BEK header), the entry and value type codes, the protection and
encryption-method tables, the choice of a valid metadata copy by CRC-32, the
metadata validation hash check, the key stretch for passwords and recovery
passwords, recovery-password parsing, startup-key parsing, the clear-key path,
the FVEK unpacking for 128-bit Elephant, and the device-mapper segment layout
of BITLK_activate_by_volume_key (zero segments, relocated volume header, gap
filling, activation checks). The sector transforms are not in these files:
AES-XTS and AES-CBC come from IEEE 1619 and the BitLocker IV definition, the
Elephant diffuser from Niels Ferguson, "AES-CBC + Elephant diffuser: A Disk
Encryption Algorithm for Windows Vista", Microsoft, August 2006.
