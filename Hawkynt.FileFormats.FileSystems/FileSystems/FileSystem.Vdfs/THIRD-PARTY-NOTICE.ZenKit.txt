ZenKit (GothicKit) — VDF layout and Union ZippedStream framing, converted to C#

Source:   https://github.com/GothicKit/ZenKit (src/Vfs.cc, src/Stream.cc)
Revision: e1c1044e4e0708b05db152ede44393f78d935ad5
Author:   GothicKit Contributors
Licence:  MIT (reproduced below)

VdfsReader, VdfsWriter, VdfsCatalog and VdfsZippedStream implement the volume
layout ZenKit reads and writes: the 296-byte header, the 80-byte catalog
entries with their directory and last-entry flags, and the ZippedStream block
framing. They are a conversion into this codebase, not a copy. The signatures,
field offsets, flag values (0x80000000, 0x40000000, 0x50, 0xA0) and the 8 KiB
block size are copied exactly, as the format requires. docs/VDFS-ON-DISK.md
records what was measured on real volumes.
