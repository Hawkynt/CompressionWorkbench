LfsReader.cs is converted from NetBSD's LFS on-disk definitions:

  https://github.com/NetBSD/src  sys/ufs/lfs/lfs.h (v1.222), sys/ufs/lfs/lfs_cksum.c
  (trunk, fetched 2026-11-06)

LfsVolume.cs and LfsEditor.cs are converted from NetBSD's LFS userland writer:

  https://github.com/NetBSD/src  sbin/newfs_lfs/make_lfs.c (v1.61) — volume geometry
  sbin/fsck_lfs/segwrite.c (v1.50) — partial-segment layout, segment accounting, checksums
  sbin/fsck_lfs/pass0.c, pass5.c (v1.39) — the free-list and space bookkeeping rules
  (trunk, fetched 2026-11-06)
